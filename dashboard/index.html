System notes

The dashboard polls every 30 seconds only while visible. Returning to the tab triggers an immediate fetch. In-flight requests are cancelled when the page unloads.
Live tables and date groups are keyed and updated in place so background polling does not unnecessarily destroy DOM nodes, focus, or expanded date sections.
The dashboard is a monitoring interface, not a market-data guarantee. Candle and price freshness depends on the provider, cache TTLs and rate limits. Always verify important trading decisions against your authoritative market feed.
