System notes

The dashboard polls every 30 seconds only while visible. Returning to the tab triggers an immediate fetch. In-flight requests are cancelled on page unload.
Live tables and date groups are keyed and updated in place so background polling does not unnecessarily destroy DOM nodes, focus or expanded date sections.
This is a monitoring and paper-trading dashboard, not a guaranteed market-data or execution feed. Always verify important signals and prices against the authoritative feed you trade from.
